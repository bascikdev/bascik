date: 2026-02-03

tags: [guides]
---

Create `content/posts/<slug>.md`. The file name becomes the URL, so use lowercase words joined by hyphens.

Required front matter: `title`, `description`, and `date` (YYYY-MM-DD). Optional: `updated`, `tags`, and `draft: true`, which shows the post in the dev server and leaves it out of production builds.
