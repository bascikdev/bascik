# Privacy

bascik.dev sets no cookies, shows no ads, and runs no trackers. This page lists the little it does collect and why. It covers bascik.dev and its preview URLs only.

## Analytics

bascik.dev uses [Cloudflare Web Analytics](https://www.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/) to count page views. It is cookieless: it sets no cookies, stores nothing in your browser, does not fingerprint your device, and does not follow you across sites or visits. It reports aggregate figures such as which pages were viewed, where visits came from, and how fast pages loaded.

We use it to learn which parts of the docs people read, so we can improve them. Cloudflare processes the request data, including your IP address, to produce those figures; see [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

## Hosting

The site is served by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare handles the details of each request (such as your IP address and browser) to deliver the page and protect the site from abuse.

## What Your Browser Keeps

When you switch between the light and dark theme, the site remembers your choice in your browser's `sessionStorage`, under the key `theme`. It stays on your device, is never sent anywhere, and is cleared when you close the tab.

## Other Sites

Links to GitHub, the merch store at shop.bascik.dev, and other sites lead to services with their own privacy policies.
