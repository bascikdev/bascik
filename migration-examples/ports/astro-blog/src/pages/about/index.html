This site is a hand port of the official Astro blog example to Bascik. It exists to check, one behavior at a time, what carries over from Astro and what needs a different approach.

Every route here has a counterpart in the original: a home page, a blog index, an about page, five posts, an RSS feed, and a sitemap. The visual design follows the original stylesheet. The words and pictures are new.

Nothing on these pages needs JavaScript to read. The one script on the site is the click handler on the embedded component in the Markdown post, which is there to prove that inline event attributes survive the build.
